Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026. This policy describes how Feature Space handles personal data for our services and apps, including EB Store Browser.

Data use

What we process and why.

  • We process personal data needed to operate our services and apps.
  • Depending on usage, this may include email address, consent choices, notification settings, interaction events, and limited technical identifiers from Apple services.
  • Data is used to provide core functionality, send optional product updates, send required service notices, improve reliability and security, and respond to support requests.
Sharing and retention

How data is handled operationally.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Data may be shared only with processors and infrastructure providers acting on our instructions, or with authorities when required by law.
  • Data is retained only as long as needed for the purposes above or to meet legal obligations.
Your rights

How to contact us about privacy.

If applicable law gives you rights of access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, or portability, you can contact us via our contact email.

By using our app and managing consent choices, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
